<br><br>We showed IVOR at Midwest Gaming Classic 2025 in Milwaukee, from April 4 to 6.  It was my first convention ever, and it was great to do with MoonMuse, only about three months after I joined the team.  We bring retro televisions and monitors to evoke the feeling of the era of games we take inspiration from, as well as to draw attention.
<br><br>Most of my last few weeks before the show went into getting the build ready.  I finished the melee troll and the golem, which now moves with root motion, and fixed a long list of smaller enemy bugs.  The main menu got its 3D background in time, along with a high score screen that cycles through the scores so they're easy to read from a distance.
<br><br>The final two days were sound.  I wired up sound effects for the trolls, the golem, and the Haugbui, added impact and break sounds for objects, and made the audio dip between rounds.  My last change went in the day the doors opened.
<br><br>It was a lot of crunch, but it was worth it to see how enthralled players were.  Watching someone interact with and appreciate your work is second to none.
