In May 2025 I joined Epic Systems as a Software Developer on the supply chain team, building the inventory software hospitals will use to manage their supplies and handle recalls.  I work mostly in MUMPS and React.
<br><br>So far I've completed 25 features and fixes, 19 of which have shipped to customers, and written 24 approved technical designs across three releases.
<br><br>I have led several projects, including one that brought purchase requests into Epic's new inventory app.  I designed and built most of its features, ran performance testing up to 10,000 items, and saw it through sign-off to ship in the May 2026 release.
<br><br>I also built four of the fourteen core features in a shared recall framework used across Epic's applications, along with searching and saved column layouts in the inventory app.  On top of project work, I've delivered high-priority improvements to security, data reliability, and accessibility, including better screen reader support.
<br><br>I'm now leading development on the next phase of our inventory work.  It's pretty different from making games, but I've really enjoyed it.
