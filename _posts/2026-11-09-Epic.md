In May 2025 I joined Epic Systems as a Software Developer on the EpicOps - Supply Chain team, building the inventory software hospitals will use to manage their supplies and handle recalls.  I work across the full stack, mostly in MUMPS, C#, and React with TypeScript.
<br><br>So far I've completed many features and fixes and written dozens of approved technical designs across multiple releases.
<br><br>I've led some projects, including one that brought purchase requests into Epic's new inventory app.  I designed and built most of its features, ran performance testing to handle a very high number of items, and saw it through sign-off to ship in the May 2026 release.
<br><br>I also built core features in a shared recall framework used across Epic's applications, along with searching and saved column layouts in the inventory app.  I've worked in Epic's reporting databases, Clarity and Caboodle, as well, using SQL to build tables, data exports, and logging of how end users use features.  On top of project work, I've delivered high-priority improvements to security, data reliability, and accessibility, including better screen reader support.
<br><br>I'm now leading development on the next phase of our inventory work.  It's pretty different from making games, but I've really enjoyed it.  It's just a different kind of challenge, and I know my work is helping people.
