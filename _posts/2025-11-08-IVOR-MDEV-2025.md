<a href="https://www.mdevconf.com/" target="_blank">M+DEV</a>
<br><br>We brought IVOR to M+DEV 2025 in Madison on November 7 and 8, the second showcase of the year after Midwest Gaming Classic in April.  It was my first time at M+DEV, and it was cool being at an industry conference, albeit a small one.  Meeting up with my remote dev team to spend time together is always a pleasure, and it always gets us excited to implement new features and build new things for the game.
<br><br>The build for M+DEV 2025 was the first one to show off the work I'd done on enemies through the fall.  They patrol, notice you, and get aggressive, and when they die they drop as ragdolls instead of always bursting apart.  This increased interactivity was noticeable in how much fun people were having playing.
<br><br>The last week before the show was fixes and sound, which is how it seems to usually go.  I added and reworked sounds for the enemies and weapons, gave the golem a smash attack, and changed the line-of-sight check so the cannon trolls stop shooting at you through walls.
