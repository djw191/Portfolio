<br><br>IVOR was back at Midwest Gaming Classic 2026 in Milwaukee from April 24 to 26, the show's 25th anniversary and my second year there.  It started feeling more routine, and I gained a lot of confidence in the lead-up, both in development and con prep.  We brought a brand new backsplash banner and matching company shirts with our updated logo.
<br><br>We completed a new build of the game for it.  My part in the month before was mostly polish.  I got the fog and post-processing right for the new water, including when you go underwater, and tracked down the sounds that weren't routed through the right audio group so the volume sliders control everything.  The rebinding menu also now shows controller button icons instead of text.
