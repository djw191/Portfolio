<br><br>On July 31, 2026 the IVOR demo launched on Steam, and most of my work in the months before it was about getting the game ready for players.
<br><br>Early in the year I tuned combat a bit.  Crossbow bolts now have a chance to break on impact, and the sniper enemies got simpler and more aggressive.
<br><br>In the spring I added water to the game by integrating and adapting Unity's water system, including getting the fog and post-processing right when you go underwater.  I also tracked down every sound that wasn't routed through the right audio group, so the volume sliders actually control everything.
<br><br>I finished showing real controller button icons in the rebinding menu and made rebinding consistent across devices.  I added navigation areas so certain enemies stay in the parts of a level they belong in, fixed patrol speeds, and kept extending the breakables system.  I also built an editor tool that finds shadow-casting lights that aren't covered by the game's light zones, so none slip past the system that keeps lighting costs down.
