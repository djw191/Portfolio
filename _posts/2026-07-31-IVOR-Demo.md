<br><br>The IVOR demo is finally out on Steam!
<br><br>Most of my work in the months leading up to it was about putting final polish on a lot of things and making sure changes were made based on player and tester feedback.
<br><br>Early in the year I tuned combat a bit, giving crossbow bolts a chance to break on impact (but sometimes be recoverable as ammo), and changing the cannon trolls to be simpler and more aggressive, as players found them odd.  Ranged enemies now fire at where they last saw you for a time, and small random speed differences keep groups from moving in lockstep.
<br><br>In the spring I added a new, better-looking water effect to the game by integrating and adapting the water system from one of Unity's demo projects, including getting the fog and post-processing to look right when you go underwater.  I also tracked down every sound that wasn't routed through the right audio group, so the volume sliders actually control everything.  The rebinding menu now shows the actual controller button icons instead of text, which makes it much easier to read at a glance.  That same spring we completed a build of the game for Midwest Gaming Classic 2026.  This build is almost the same as the demo we shipped.
<br><br>After that I added navigation areas so certain enemies stay in the parts of a level they belong in, fixed patrol speeds, and kept extending the breakables system.
<br><br>Just before MGC I also built an editor tool that finds shadow-casting lights that aren't covered by the game's light zones, so none slip past the system that keeps lighting costs down.  Light zones allow the level designer to cover an area with a volume and designate lights that should be on when the player is there.  This allows us to save on lighting and shadow costs for lights that the player wouldn't see or notice.
<br><br>Launch day itself was still pretty nerve-racking despite all the prep and the version we built for MGC 2026.  However, it was also exciting.  Brandon, one of the studio's founders, almost couldn't look after we hit the publish button.
