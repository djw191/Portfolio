This was a project for my advanced 3D modeling class, where we focused on sculpting models.  This helmet model is based on Master Chief's from Halo 3.  I modeled it in Blender using traditional poly-modeling, and then moved it to ZBrush and added details.  The basic texturing was also done in ZBrush.
