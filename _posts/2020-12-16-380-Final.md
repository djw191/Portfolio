<br><br>This was the final project for my Art Studio 380 (3D Modeling) course.  I chose to model a space craft based on a sketch I had made.  I modeled it in Maya, but I mostly use Blender now.  It was textured and mapped using Substance Painter.  I used Blender's Cycles renderer for the final render seen at the top.
