I'm a Software Developer at Epic Systems, where I work on the supply chain team building the inventory software hospitals will use to manage their supplies and handle recalls.  I've led projects from design through release and built core pieces of a shared recall framework.  You can read more in [my post about Epic]({{ site.baseurl }}/blog/Epic/).  

In my spare time I'm a volunteer programmer at MoonMuse Entertainment LLC, working on gameplay, enemy AI, optimization, and lighting for [*IVOR*](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3033980/IVOR/), a Unity first-person shooter that released a demo in 2026.  You can follow along in [my IVOR posts]({{ site.baseurl }}/tags/#ivor).

I attended UW-Whitewater, graduating Magna Cum Laude with a Bachelor of Science (B.S.) in Computer Science and Media Arts and Game Development, and went on to earn a Master of Science (M.S.) in Computer Science from UW-Milwaukee in 2024.
