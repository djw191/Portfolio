I'm a full-stack Software Developer at Epic Systems, where I work on the EpicOps - Supply Chain team building the inventory software hospitals will use to manage their supplies and handle recalls.  I've led projects from design through release and built core pieces of a shared recall framework, along with reporting tables and data exports in Clarity and Caboodle.  You can read more in [my post about Epic]({{ site.baseurl }}/blog/Epic/).  

In my spare time I'm a contract gameplay programmer at MoonMuse Entertainment LLC, working on gameplay, enemy AI, editor tools, optimization, and lighting for [*IVOR*](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3033980/IVOR/), a Unity first-person shooter that released a demo in 2026.  I've also helped bring it to four conventions: Midwest Gaming Classic in Milwaukee and M+DEV in Madison, in both 2025 and 2026.  You can follow along in [my IVOR posts]({{ site.baseurl }}/tags/#ivor).

I attended UW-Whitewater, graduating Magna Cum Laude with a Bachelor of Science (B.S.) in Computer Science and Media Arts and Game Development, and went on to earn a Master of Science (M.S.) in Computer Science from UW-Milwaukee in 2024.
